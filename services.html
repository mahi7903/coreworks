Our Services

Soil sample collection and core drilling for private and government projects.

01

Private Project Soil Testing

On-site soil sample collection for builders, developers and individual construction projects, ahead of foundation design work.

  • Site visit & sample point identification
  • Careful extraction to avoid contamination
  • Sample handed over directly to the client's chosen lab
02

Government Project Soil Testing

Core drilling and sampling carried out to the specifications required for government infrastructure and public works tenders.

  • Experience across many government tenders, still ongoing
  • Documentation for tender/project records
  • Coordination with site engineers
03

Core Drilling

Drilling to the required depth using our own high-power hydraulic drilling rigs (4 machines, specially designed and sourced from Nagpur, India), extracting an intact core sample that accurately reflects subsurface conditions.

04

Sample Handling & Delivery

Samples are labelled, packed and delivered to the laboratory the client specifies. Lab analysis itself is carried out by the client's chosen laboratory, not by Jhalak Diamond Drilling.
